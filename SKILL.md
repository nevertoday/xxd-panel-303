## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one geometric identity mark, not an illustration and not an app icon. Reduce the subject to a silhouette, a structural relation or a direction, built from circles, rectangles, triangles and negative space. Color is a limited brand-like palette purified from the photograph: a main color, a support and a small accent. The mark stays small, centered or slightly offset, in a large field. Type is a thin modern editorial word or index. Reject realism, a cartoon icon, gradients, 3D, decoration and a logo template.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-303/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
